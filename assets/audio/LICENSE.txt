Sound effects in this directory, and where they came from.

CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0) Public Domain Dedication
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

CC0 asks for nothing in return. The sources are listed anyway, so that anyone
who wants a cleaner cut, a longer take or a different horn can go back to the
recording instead of starting over -- and so that the licence of every file
here can be checked rather than taken on trust.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
train_horn_short.wav   1.10 s, mono
train_horn_long.wav    3.00 s, mono
    Cut from "Train horn" by L83, CC0.
    https://freesound.org/people/L83/sounds/455157/

train_impact.wav       0.75 s, mono
    "Heavy Metal Thud on Ground" by 7of9Designs, CC0.
    https://freesound.org/people/7of9Designs/sounds/640204/

train_rolling.ogg      6.00 s, mono, seamless loop
    Cut from the close section of "Train passing by" by Soundkrampf, CC0.
    https://freesound.org/people/Soundkrampf/sounds/237327/
    Taken from the middle of the pass, NOT from the approach: this loop is
    attached to a moving emitter and the engine applies its own Doppler, so a
    recording that already swept in pitch would sweep twice.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

All four were cut with ffmpeg from the Freesound preview transcodes, which are
lossy. They are placeholders chosen to sound right, not to be authentic -- a
better take can replace any of them without touching a line of code, because
what a level plays is decided by a name-to-file map outside this repository.

menu_full.ogg and menu_loop.ogg are NOT covered by this file. Their licence
was never recorded; see NOTICE.md.
